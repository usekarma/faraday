## Investigation proposal: oracle feasibility before comparator work

**Question:** Can an independent labeling procedure and access boundary distinguish decision-preserving representation from material drift and unknown semantics?

**Competing hypotheses / inferred:** (a) independently specified contracts and evidence suffice; (b) labels merely encode the favored comparator or the known relay exception; (c) necessary operational meaning is unavailable, so only UNKNOWN is honest; (d) a simple strict/checklist policy is sufficient for the present decision and comparator research adds no value.

**Apparatus/data proposed:** Q-004, EXP-004's already disclosed case families, O-001/E-003, portable source references, declared decision contracts, and a human-designated independent custodian/reviewer. This cycle supplies no new corpus, oracle labels, held-out cases, exception set, or comparator code. A future feasibility review should assess the procedure, not reveal comparator-evaluation material.

**Controls proposed:** distinguish contract conformance from operational-effect equivalence; require a source-backed rationale independent of comparator outputs; preserve label disagreements as UNKNOWN; compare ordinary strict/checklist semantics with scoped semantics; distinguish exact-version versus bytes-only artifact contracts; treat a naming correction as a specification change; require applicability scope for null/empty equivalence. Preserve EXP-004's six independent commitments and pre-authoring freeze/reveal rules in any later approved implementation. A hash does not certify labels or independence.

**Expected observations proposed:** a feasible independent evidence/custody plan; a narrower defensible scope with unsupported families assigned UNKNOWN; or documented inability to establish independent labels/roles. These are possible outcomes, not observed results. The reviewer must report missing evidence and prior exposure rather than infer that blinding holds from timestamps.

**Falsification criterion proposed:** the feasibility claim fails if label rationale depends on comparator output, disagreement must be forced into a non-UNKNOWN label, scope is justified only by the single incident while presented as general, or independent access/freeze ordering cannot be established. Report infeasible/inconclusive rather than tune labels to produce acceptance. This does not falsify H-002's empirical performance claim; it prevents claiming a valid test of it.

**Interpretation limits:** a feasible plan is not an executed blind experiment, verified semantic equivalence, physical identity, safety finding, care, or alignment. Passing future fixture evaluation would still establish only bounded behavior. No outcome grants permission to implement downstream work.

**Conditional repository involvement:** Faraday owns assessment and experiment direction; aws-iac supplies verifier/incident scope, aws-config desired-state contracts, aws-lambda runtime contracts, iot-digital-twin apparatus constraints. Karma, Adage, and Fabric roles remain those in the ecosystem registry; do not invent changes to their repositories.
