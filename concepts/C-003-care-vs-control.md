# C-003 — Care versus control

**Status:** SPECULATIVE

## Intuition

Care may involve becoming more predictable where another agent's safety or vulnerability matters while preserving that agent's meaningful future choices elsewhere.

## Important warning

Low entropy, high mutual information, or strong behavioral coupling are not sufficient evidence of care. A coercive or manipulative controller can also be highly predictable and highly responsive.

## Candidate distinction

```text
control: reduce another agent's degrees of freedom
care:    reduce relevant danger while preserving meaningful agency
```

This formulation needs operational definitions and empirical testing.
