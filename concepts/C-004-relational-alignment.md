# C-004 — Relational alignment

**Status:** SPECULATIVE

## Intuition

Alignment may be usefully studied as a property of a coupled human–AI–environment system rather than solely as a hidden property inside the model.

Candidate observables include correctability, preservation of human options, sensitivity to relevant human state, measurable consequences, recovery after disagreement, and resistance to manipulation of the oversight process.

## Counterpressure

A relational metric can still be gamed. Observable behavior does not by itself solve deceptive alignment, evaluation awareness, or specification failure.
