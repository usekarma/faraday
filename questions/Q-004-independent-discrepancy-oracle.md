## Competing explanations and failure criterion

The oracle may have independent contract support; it may merely encode the author's favored comparator; or the necessary semantics may require live evidence currently unavailable. A policy assertion can define a contract but cannot establish that two representations have the same operational effects. The feasibility claim fails if labels require comparator outcomes, ambiguous cases must be forced into EQUIVALENT/MATERIAL, no independent role/access boundary is feasible, or all equivalences merely restate one incident without scope limits.
