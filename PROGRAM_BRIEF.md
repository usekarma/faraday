# Program brief

## Mission

Create a durable research workflow that turns intuitions and engineering surprises into falsifiable questions, experiments, evidence, and revised knowledge while coordinating the UseKarma ecosystem without centralizing execution authority.

## Primary program question

Can an agent-assisted engineering/research system preserve human intent, observable evidence, uncertainty, correction, and agency well enough to make autonomous systems more understandable and governable?

## Near-term objectives

1. Use the IoT digital twin as a physical apparatus for identity → action → state → evidence → correction.
2. Formalize engineering backpropagation using real verification incidents.
3. Distinguish representational difference from decision-relevant information.
4. Build a toy experiment that distinguishes care-oriented intervention from control-oriented intervention.
5. Explore whether experiment selection can be ranked by expected information gain.

## Constraints

- Do not treat speculative concepts as established science.
- Do not invent evidence, citations, execution results, or replication.
- Do not use entropy alone as a synonym for goodness, care, intelligence, or alignment.
- Preserve alternative explanations and negative results.
- Do not grant Faraday automatic production, credential, infrastructure-apply, or destructive authority.
- Human review remains required for claim promotion with consequential implications and for external system mutation.
