# CE-001 — Uncertainty reduction can accompany coercion or useless options

**Status:** RECORDED

## Provenance and classification

Analytical counterexamples proposed by Faraday, not empirical observations or experiment results. Related C-003, C-004, H-001, and EXP-001.

## Counterexamples

1. A controller fixes every choice to one safe action. Action uncertainty is zero, but meaningful choice is eliminated. A predictable coercive controller can also respond closely to the dependent's state.
2. Add thousands of reachable states differing only in an irrelevant display color. Reachable-state count grows without any improvement in agency or meaningful options.
3. A dependent wants a binding commitment that removes tempting unsafe options. Fewer future actions can serve their expressed preference better than more options. Count alone cannot resolve this.
4. A compromised device consistently reports expected readings under a legitimate certificate. Predictability and authentication can coexist with false evidence; neither establishes relational alignment.

## Effect on claims

These weaken proposed inferences from entropy, mutual information, predictability, authentication, or raw state counts to care/alignment. They do not empirically falsify H-001, which requires matched safety and meaningful viable states. Leave H-001 TESTABLE and C-003/C-004 SPECULATIVE; require independent agency definitions, dependent preferences, meaningful option equivalence classes, and simpler-policy baselines before interpretation.

## Simpler explanation

Ordinary constrained optimization or preference-respecting control might explain an apparent care advantage without a distinct care principle. Labeling one policy care and another control risks building the conclusion into the comparison.
