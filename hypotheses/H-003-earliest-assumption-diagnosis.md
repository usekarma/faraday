# H-003 — Backward tracing improves diagnosis of incorrect assumptions

**Status:** TESTABLE

## Claim

On independently labeled multi-layer incident cases, structured tracing from discrepancy through verifier, implementation, architecture, specification, and assumption identifies an earliest causally relevant incorrect assumption more accurately than final-component patching, and adds decision value over ordinary root-cause analysis under the same evidence and time budget.

## Source and variables

C-001; E-003's representation failure, draft-contract correction, and rule-name correction motivate the claim. Measure diagnosis agreement with an independent intervention-based oracle, repair scope, recurrence on held-out variants, time, and unsupported causal assertions. Multiple equally valid causes must be labeled as such rather than forced into one origin.

## Competing explanation and falsification

This may simply rename root-cause analysis. If ordinary root-cause analysis matches or improves accuracy and recurrence outcomes at comparable cost, the incremental-value claim is weakened. If no independent earliest-cause oracle can be specified, do not call the claim tested. A retrospective persuasive narrative is insufficient.

## Status

New testable formulation; no comparative experiment completed. Does not establish literal backpropagation or automatic learning. This is not the selected next experiment.
